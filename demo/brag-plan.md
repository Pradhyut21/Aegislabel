# BRAG Plan: AegisLabel-FX

Workflow source: https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag, MIT. Narration requested, so the Kokoro voice route is enabled. Output is a real-app demonstration, not an animated reconstruction of app results.

## Nine-question rubric

1. App: change-safe device-label revision, verification and version-bound human review.
2. Strongest grounded claim: a warning unchanged from the previous artwork can violate an updated approved specification, and the rules block it.
3. Hook: actual red BLOCKED finding beside the source and observed warning.
4. UI to show: actual change assessment, three-panel Studio, blocked finding, corrected draft, separate review, release download, executed Mutation Lab and audit verification.
5. Shortest satisfying demonstration: approximately two minutes. Intentional exception to BRAG's short promo default: six working stages, evidence inspection and explicit prototype limits need readable holds. The official hackathon limit is pending; no eligibility claim is made.
6. Tone: polished; calm, precise engineering walkthrough with restrained transitions.
7. Audio: free local Kokoro narration, voice af_heart. Clearly labeled AI narration. No cloning, paid generation, music bed or noisy effects; voice-only keeps critical wording intelligible and avoids unrelated media rights.
8. Share copy: A warning changed. The artwork missed it. AegisLabel-FX blocks the mismatch, links the evidence and requires a fresh exact-version review before release.
9. Flow: source update → impacted labels → faulty draft blocked → corrected draft validated → reviewer approval → real release ZIP, followed by separate mutation and audit checks.

## Audio constraints

No music: intentional speech-first engineering demo. No SFX: real interactions and clear narration are sufficient. Beat/cue synchronization and audio-reactive decoration are not used. Target integrated loudness approximately -16 LUFS and peaks below -1 dBTP. Check duration, final audio stream and loudness; do not claim a human performed it.
